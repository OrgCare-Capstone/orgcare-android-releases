## Features

### Organization & Member Management
- Manage student organizations, members, officers, and volunteers.
- Simplify member imports through Google Classroom.
- Organize leadership roles and participation.

### Events & Attendance
- Create and manage organizational events.
- Record attendance using QR codes or Student IDs.
- Access previously loaded QR codes without an internet connection.
- Reduce proxy attendance through QR code sharing restrictions.

### Offline-First Experience
- Record attendance even without internet connectivity.
- Automatically synchronize offline attendance records when connectivity returns.
- Keep essential attendance workflows available offline.

### Certificates & Recognition
- Create and customize event certificates.
- Manage certificate generation and distribution.
- Recognize student participation and achievements.

### Analytics & Reporting
- Monitor member engagement and event participation.
- Explore organizational insights and analytics.
- Generate attendance records and reports.

## Installation Guide

1. Visit the [Latest Releases](../../releases/latest) page.
2. Download the latest `OrgCare-v1.2-build8.apk` file.
3. Open the downloaded APK on your Android device.
4. If prompted, allow installation from your browser or file manager.
5. Complete the installation and launch OrgCare.
6. Sign in with your account to get started.

**Note:** Download APK files only from this official repository or other trusted OrgCare distribution channels.

## About OrgCare

OrgCare is designed to support student organizations by simplifying day-to-day operations, improving participation tracking, and making organizational management more accessible.

From managing memberships to organizing events, tracking attendance, generating certificates, and reviewing engagement analytics, OrgCare provides a unified experience for students and organization leaders.
